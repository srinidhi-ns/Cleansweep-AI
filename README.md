# CleanSweep AI: Automated Urban Waste Detection & Dynamic Municipal Dispatch

> **Automated Waste Detection, Dynamic Municipal Dispatch & Civic Enforcement**  
> *Transforming urban waste management through AI vision, smart logistics, and automated deterrence.*

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**CleanSweep AI** addresses urban waste accumulation challenges caused by two primary operational gaps:
1. **Detection & Routing Deficit:** Lack of continuous automated mechanisms to detect unauthorized garbage piles, leaving sanitation crews dependent on slow manual reports and inefficient patrol routes.
2. **Deterrence Deficit:** Absence of scalable enforcement mechanisms to deter repeat waste dumping at public hotspots.

CleanSweep AI creates a closed-loop system connecting transit-mounted cameras, citizen mobile uploads, AI verification, dynamic route planning, and automated penalty notices.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 📷 **Multi-Source Data Capture:** Leverages cameras mounted on public transit buses and municipal trucks during regular routes, alongside citizen photo uploads.
* 🧠 **AI Verification & OCR Engine:** Powered by the **Gemini API** to verify waste accumulation, log precise GPS coordinates and timestamps, and read vehicle license plates automatically using OCR.
* 🚛 **Dynamic Municipal Dispatch:** Automatically routes the nearest available garbage truck directly to verified locations, saving time and fuel.
* ⏱️ **Sub-2-Hour Resolution SLA:** Establishes a North Star target to resolve reported waste accumulation in under 2 hours with public tracking.
* ⚖️ **Automated Civic Enforcement:** Matches license plates with vehicle registry records to issue instant penalty notices to registered owners.
* 📊 **Hotspot Analytics:** Tracks repeat waste accumulation sites to optimize regular truck patrols or place permanent dustbins.

## 📊 Traditional vs. CleanSweep AI Workflow

| Dimension | Traditional Municipal Workflow | CleanSweep AI Framework |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dumpsite Detection** | Manual citizen calls & random truck patrols | Automated transit camera AI & citizen uploads |
| **Dispatch Routing** | Static schedule; search routes driven aimlessly | Dynamic routing sends nearest crew straight to GPS tag |
| **Resolution SLA** | Unpredictable delays (days to weeks); no timer | Strict < 2 Hour resolution window with public tracking |
| **Capital Expenditure** | High cost requiring permanent pole camera installation | Low setup cost leveraging existing transit fleet cameras |
| **Civic Enforcement** | Manual inspections; rare enforcement | Automated OCR license plate lookup & instant fines |
